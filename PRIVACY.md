# Privacy

- Camera capture begins only after the user clicks Start Signal.
- Camera frames and hand landmarks used for gesture recognition stay in the
  active browser tab and are not uploaded or stored.
- Stop, unmount, and capture failure close tracks and clear live state.
- Teach by Demo is a separate explicit workflow. It keeps raw recording local
  and may send 6–10 disclosed compressed keyframes to produce a reviewed plan.
- Draft commands and profiles remain in browser storage until exported or
  published.
- Public profiles are unlisted and redacted. They contain secret references,
  never secret values.
- Planner text is sent only when Generate is chosen. Browser commands are
  previewed and validated before save or execution.
